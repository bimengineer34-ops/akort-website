Redesign homepage hero as full-bleed photo with overlaid text/CTAs

Matches the pattern used by most large engineering-firm homepages
(Arup, WSP, AECOM, ...): a full-bleed background photo with a dark
gradient for legibility, headline/subtitle/buttons layered on top -
instead of the previous template default of a centered text block
followed by a separate image stacked underneath it.

- Hero.astro: image now renders as an absolutely-positioned
  background covering the section (object-cover) with a
  bottom-to-top black gradient overlay; text/tagline/subtitle switch
  to white/light colors (fixed, not theme-dependent, since they now
  sit on a photo rather than the page background); non-primary action
  buttons get a white-outline override so they read against the photo.
- New hero photo: a wide aerial shot of a site team on a completed
  foundation slab (clean PPE, no legible logos) - replaces the
  blueprint/pencil macro shot, which doesn't crop well as a wide
  full-bleed background.
- Header contrast fix: the header is transparent until scrolled 60px,
  which left the (fixed-color, non-recolorable) logo icon low-contrast
  against the new dark photo. Added a `solid` prop to Header.astro
  (and a `solidHeader` passthrough on PageLayout) that always shows
  the existing solid/blurred background band instead of only after
  scrolling - enabled only for the homepage, every other page's header
  is unchanged.

Verified in light/dark mode and on mobile.
